Moments

Sam

She was just amazing, and I love her, always will.

Tanoushka

She used to open her yellow umbrella in such a funny, dramatic way. She would hold it like a club and to open it, she would forcefully extend her arm so the umbrella stem would slide open. Then she would dramatically press the button and the umbrella, as if following her cue, would burst open.

Sam

I remember the first time I texted you, the day you joined our college group chat. I saw your username, Mavis, and messaged you privately: “Mavis? like the character from Fairy Tail?” You replied straight away, “OMG how did you know that”.

And since that conversation we were connected, because of all our shared interests and of course our undying love for Avatar the Last Airbender. We talked everyday, called each other, played games with each other. We did anything and everything but do online classes.

Ananya

I find her flair for languages very impressive. When I first met her, I was telling her how I was learning Korean and somehow she knew how to read and write in the Korean script. Of course, she was into Japanese. She also knew a lot of French. She could recognise German and even Swedish.

Once when we had gone to IKEA she just pronounced every word right and would even correct my pronunciation. I asked her how she knew Swedish of all languages, and she just shrugged and said she just knows a bit of some languages and Swedish just happens to be on that list.

Tanoushka

Our first interim together was the first time we met. I was sitting with someone else the first day. On the second day Mavis asks me in an annoyed voice, “why didn’t you save me a seat??????” and I was a little flabbergasted, because I thought she would have lots of friends she would rather sit with.

I pulled up an extra chair for her, but found her crouching over her iPad on the ground, drawing something. I asked her if she wanted to sit, but she was already very comfortable on the ground. She would give immaculate answers crouching and being virtually invisible to the rest of the class. I found her so fascinating that day and still do to this day.

Sam

My favourite memory is when we were doing blind karaoke on our phones. I said “Idk which song this is, play Motion Sickness by Phoebe Bridgers and I’ll know!!” She says “bro hahaha I can’t, I need to record this! just try to guess” and starts recording. I say I don’t knowwww which song that is, and then when the round ends she turns her phone and shows me the name, Motion Sickness by Phoebe Bridgers. We laughed so hard.

Ananya

There are sooo many, but the one I’m thinking of is when we had a sleepover at my house. Season 3 of Squid Game had just come out, so we were binge watching it. We had lunch the next day at Marine Drive and it was such a lovely time with her, as always.

I always thought she was very clever and had so much random information about everything. She really sounded like an encyclopedia.

Tanoushka

The second time we got to be in the same interim class was such a special moment in my life. I get a notification from mail and a text from Mavis saying “LMAOOOO HELLO” at the same time. In exhilaration I open the mail first, I see her name, and I remember getting so ecstatic that we get to be with each other for a whole month again.

I reply saying “SAME. INTERIM???” She says “YES”, and that we should “keep the streak”.

Sam

Her impressions and voice acting skills were so fascinating, she’d mimic characters way too well. She’d do this silly thing of swinging open the lift door herself and then shouting “ladies first” in a really sassy way before entering, and holding the door for me.

Tanoushka

Mavis was a very goofy person, but couldn’t stand it when jokes were disrespectful. This one time we were having a live funeral in our interim class and someone was making jokes about the funeral. We rolled our eyes, and Mavis stopped working after a point out of anger.

She was a very principled person. I will always admire that about her.

Sam

Even though you struggled so much everyday, you never made anyone feel any less seen.

You raised countless funds for families and people in need. You made comics about your own life hoping it would help someone out there, and it helped, and saved countless people, and made an incredibly safe space for them. Me included.

Sam

I’ll remember you. We’ll remember you, with all the happy memories. Memories of you drawing all over our tables, all over our sketchbooks, making the most creatively hilarious jokes.

I’ll think of all of these and remember all the happy memories we shared together, and maybe have some popcorn and Pepsi and watch some Avatar the Last Airbender.
