The Sky

A poem

The sky, it is so high up,
And I can’t reach it at all.
I can’t think, I’m stuck.
Every time I fall.

I look at the stars,
And I see my dreams.
The things I want,
The things unseen.

What’s the point,
of the sky so high?
If I can’t even see it
With my own eyes?

What is the point
Of anything if
the world ends one day?
But knowing me,
And my thoughts,
Who am I to say?

Even if, our lives didn’t matter,
We’re here right now.
Despite the logic and the long run,
We’re here right now.

The sky so high,
And I can’t reach it,
Life’s big mystery,
And I can’t teach it.

The sky is so unbelievably high,
But I don’t need to go there yet.
Because I have all I need to live;
All of the people that I’ve met.

Each of them helps me,
Good or bad.
They make me stronger,
And make me glad.

All the smiles I’ve shared
On this familiar ground.
All the sadness,
And it’s echoing sound.

If I can’t find my pillar,
I’ll be one instead.
I’ll be the stone
On which I rest my head.

So treasure my chances,
All the moments I’ve had.
That the world gave them to me,
Even if it made me mad

The sky so high,
I like it from here.
Someday when I’ll fly,
Up there I’ll appear.

At last all I can say,
At the end of the day.
No matter how I deprived,
I lived; I was alive

Ananya & Mavi
